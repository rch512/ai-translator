**Translation:**

Coffee on a Rainy Day

It was a quiet Tuesday afternoon. A fine rain was falling softly over the streets of Tokyo. Kenji, a university student, dashed into a retro coffee shop near the station to escape the sudden rain.

Inside, a pleasant aroma hung in the air, as if roasted coffee beans and old books had mingled together. Behind the counter, an elderly master was quietly wiping a cup. Kenji ordered a warm house-blend coffee and opened the paperback he had just bought.

As he turned the pages, a calico cat wandered in through the slightly open back door. The master smiled gently, poured milk into a small saucer, and set it on the floor. Watching the peaceful scene of the cat lapping up the milk, Kenji murmured to himself, "Getting caught in the rain isn't so bad after all."
